HOW TO READ THE STATUS LINE
  CONFIRMED    Name, year, contributors and link were checked online.
  PARTLY       Some details are confirmed. The note says what is still open.
  UNCONFIRMED  Taken from the project files only, or not found online.

"Service address" means the link is copied from the project code. It was not
opened during this check. Live datasets have no single publication year, so
their year says "Live service, used 2026".

Everything that still needs a manual search is listed in section 6.

    Year:         About 2015 (from the project code)
    Contributors: Ajuntament de Barcelona. Possibly built by Tecnogeo (see Status).
    Used for:     Benchmark: the city's kWh, kW and usable area next to ours, roof by roof
    Link:         https://coneixement-eu.bcn.cat/mapaEnergia/
                  https://www.construible.es/2015/05/19/visor-energetico-que-identifica-recursos-renovables-para-edificios

    Status:       PARTLY. The viewer returned an error (403) during this check, so its
                  credits and date were not read. A 2015 article says Tecnogeo built a
                  matching solar tool for the Ajuntament, but it never names the Mapa
                  d'Energia, so they may not be the same tool.


3. METHOD REFERENCES
--------------------

M1. Automatic Segmentation of Raw LIDAR Data for Extraction of Building Roofs
    Year:         2014
    Contributors: Mohammad Awrangjeb, Clive S. Fraser
    Published in: Remote Sensing 6(5), 3716-3751
    Used for:     The plane-growing idea and the 0.15 m tolerance in the step 2 roof reading
    Link:         https://doi.org/10.3390/rs6053716
    Status:       CONFIRMED

M2. U-Net: Convolutional Networks for Biomedical Image Segmentation
    Year:         2015
    Contributors: Olaf Ronneberger, Philipp Fischer, Thomas Brox
    Used for:     The architecture of the step 2 AI model
    Link:         https://arxiv.org/abs/1505.04597
    Status:       CONFIRMED

M3. Deep Residual Learning for Image Recognition (ResNet)
    Year:         2015 (arXiv)
    Contributors: Kaiming He, Xiangyu Zhang, Shaoqing Ren, Jian Sun
    Used for:     The ResNet-34 encoder of the step 2 AI model
    Link:         https://arxiv.org/abs/1512.03385
    Status:       CONFIRMED

M4. Segmentation Models Pytorch
    Year:         2019
    Contributors: Pavel Iakubovskii
    Used for:     The library that builds and trains the step 2 AI model
    Link:         https://github.com/qubvel-org/segmentation_models.pytorch
    Status:       CONFIRMED

M5. ImageNet: A Large-Scale Hierarchical Image Database
    Year:         2009
    Contributors: Jia Deng et al.
    Used for:     Pretrained starting weights for the AI model's encoder
    Link:         https://doi.org/10.1109/CVPR.2009.5206848
    Status:       PARTLY. Check the full author list on the paper itself.


4. LEGAL AND COST SOURCES
-------------------------

L1. Real Decreto 244/2019 (self-consumption rules)
    Year:         2019
    Contributors: Government of Spain (BOE-A-2019-5089)
    Used for:     Shared self-consumption, 500 m distance rule, 100 kW and 15 kW limits
    Link:         https://www.boe.es/buscar/pdf/2019/BOE-A-2019-5089-consolidado.pdf
    Status:       PARTLY. The decree is confirmed. The article numbers quoted in the code
                  (3.g, 4.2.a, 7.1.b.ii) were not checked against the current text.

L2. Real Decreto-ley 7/2026 (final provision 14 amends RD 244/2019)
    Year:         2026 (20 March; in force 22 March according to an industry website)
    Contributors: Government of Spain
    Used for:     The 5,000 m option for solar up to 5 MW
    Link:         https://www.iberley.es/legislacion/disposicion-final-14-real-decreto-ley-7-2026-20-marzo-plan-integral-respuesta-crisis-oriente-medio
    Status:       PARTLY. The decree is confirmed. The 5,000 m and 5 MW details come from
                  industry websites, not from the BOE. Find the BOE text.

L3. Barcelona tax ordinances: ICIO 95% bonus (2026, art. 7.4), IBI 50% for 3 years (2025, art. 9.6)
    Year:         2025 and 2026
    Contributors: Ajuntament de Barcelona
    Used for:     Community costs and payback in step 5
    Link:         https://ajuntament.barcelona.cat/premsa/2026/01/30/aprovades-definitivament-les-ordenances-fiscals-de-2026/
    Status:       UNCONFIRMED. The ordinance text was not found. The city's announcement
                  links the 95% ICIO bonus to urban regeneration, not solar. Commercial
                  websites disagree on whether the IBI bonus lasts 3 or 5 years.

L4. OCU 2026 solar installation prices (1,330-1,500 EUR per kWp, VAT included)
    Year:         2026 (according to the project code)
    Contributors: OCU (Organización de Consumidores y Usuarios)
    Used for:     The 1,400 EUR per kWp cost in step 5
    Link:         NOT FOUND
    Status:       UNCONFIRMED. No OCU 2026 figure was found. Other 2026 websites suggest
                  about 850-1,300 EUR per kWp without a battery, which is lower.

L5. "Legal check for the rooftop solar tool"
    Year:         2026 (3 November)
    Contributors: Not recorded
    Used for:     The legal rules in pipeline/step5/communities.py
    Link:         NOT FOUND. The code cites this document, but it is not in the repository.
    Status:       UNCONFIRMED
